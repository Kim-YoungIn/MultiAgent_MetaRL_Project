## 2. Interim Scientific Takeaways (Anchored to Core Research Question)

1. **Meta-learning's impact on multi-agent exploration is sharply conditional, not monotonic:**
   - Where costly active probing is required and the opponent is stationary (`Exp 1 R3`), off-policy recurrent meta-RL with self-supervised public prediction (`drqn_aux_yr`) learns near-optimal 2-step adaptive probing (`99.7%` of exact Bayes-Opt), whereas pure-reward on-policy `rl2_ppo` collapses and `drqn_vanilla` suffers high variance on hard types (`θ=2`).
2. **Stationarity & reliability boundaries flip the advantage:**
   - Policies meta-trained on stationary opponents collapse under non-stationary online learners (`Exp 2A`) and observation noise (`Exp 3A`). Online-matched training restores risk-aware hedging (`Q-learning`, `d=0.3`), yet high OOD noise (`Expected SARSA`, `d=0.6`, `coop_trap d=0.6`) exposes overconfident exploration that underperforms blind safe floors (`prior_only`) or simple `UCB×UCB`.
3. **Inductive bias against action-label memorization unlocks multi-family transfer:**
   - Across 4 distinct semantic game families (`Exp 3B`), standard One-Hot DRQN fails to beat `UCB×UCB` even with 2× budget, whereas the exact $S_4$-equivariant recurrent package jumps `+12.6–13.6` Welfare and transfers zero-shot against UCB on an unseen 5th family (`+30.71`), even without auxiliary losses (`equiv_vanilla` achieves comparable gains).
4. **Hard multi-barrier scaling (`T=64`, 22 actions) reveals a deep exploration ceiling:**
   - Under standard $\varepsilon$-greedy warmup, neither fixed `aux_yr` nor local cosine gradient-alignment gating (`drqn_aux_discovery`) unlocks even Tier 1 (`0%` higher-tier exploitation vs `100%` for Bayes-Opt), highlighting that representation/auxiliary losses alone cannot substitute for coverage when positive delayed-confirmation trajectories are rare.
