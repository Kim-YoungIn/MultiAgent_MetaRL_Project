## 5. Publication requirements and remaining uncertainty

- Five training seeds, not thousands of episodes, govern uncertainty about training reliability. Cross-seed pairings reuse checkpoints; verify bootstrap clustering over both participating seeds rather than treating pairings as independent replicates.
- Report nominal CIs and distinguish predeclared primary comparisons from exploratory auxiliary/matchup analyses. The many contrasts do not support unrestricted selection of significant findings.
- Leakage exclusion, rule-level split uniqueness, seed provenance, and bootstrap mechanics remain unverified from these tables. Structural-model superiority/equivalence deserves prominence despite its smaller offline budget (**24,576** transitions).
- Early-adaptation AUC is absent from the permitted tables; Exp 2B IQR and Exp 1 counterpart returns are also unavailable here. Supply compact summaries before claiming the reporting contract is fully evidenced.
- Blind-trained `prior_only` does not establish equivalence to resetting a feedback-trained recurrent policy’s memory. Document this control substitution and the mask’s joint effect on inputs and auxiliary losses.
- Carry the legacy findings forward as motivation and observed failure cases, not evidence that standard multi-agent benchmarks generally fail.

## 6. Minimal falsifiable Auxiliary Task Discovery extension

1. **Scope:** Learn normalized, nonnegative weights over declared public next-observation/next-reward targets. Claim selection within this candidate set; exclude hidden task labels, true payoff tables, and Oracle outputs from learning.
2. **Ceiling gate:** R3 has only **0.055** mean return headroom and already saturated K4 values. A **+0.30** asymptotic improvement over `aux_yr` is infeasible in expectation under the existing ceiling. Use a development-locked lower-budget sample-efficiency endpoint.
3. **Minimal comparison:** learned gates, existing fixed `aux_yr`, and a development-selected fixed-weight comparator including `aux_y`/`aux_r` candidates; five independent seeds each. Match transitions, architecture, candidate normalization, and selection budgets; report extra outer-loop compute.
4. **Separation:** Optimize gates on training-only held-out episodes using extrinsic return. Lock objectives, budgets, endpoint, and analysis before fresh evaluation seeds; existing inspected panels remain historical comparators.
5. **Primary falsification:** At one locked training budget, require the paired 95% CI for cost-inclusive return improvement over the strongest static comparator to exceed **+0.30**. CI wholly inside ±0.30 is **RESOLVED_NULL**; wholly below −0.30 is **SUPPORTED_NEG**; otherwise classify by the registered four-way rule.
6. **Exploration attribution:** Require a separately registered K4 commitment/adaptive-value improvement above **0.25** where development evidence shows headroom. Return gains without this evidence support optimization/sample-efficiency claims only.
7. **Robustness:** Report mean, median, IQR, wins/ties/losses, early AUC, counterpart return, and the existing Expected-SARSA failure boundary without tuning on it. Gate magnitude or prediction accuracy alone is not success.
8. **Stop rule:** One development-selected design and one sealed comparison; no environment tuning, repeated test selection, or seed reduction. Stop early enough to protect the December 17 deliverable. Negative, null, and inconclusive outcomes remain publishable.
